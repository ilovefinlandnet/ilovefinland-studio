## 3. Step-by-Step Production Guide

### Step 1: Discover or Add a Source
1. Navigate to **Research & Sources**.
2. If an official decree or winter alert is relevant, click **`✦ Turn into Assignment`**.
3. If an old or irrelevant card appears, click **`🗑️ Delete`** to permanently clean it from your database.
4. If you have a custom Finnish government URL, click **`➕ Add Source`** to store it.

### Step 2: Establish the Assignment
1. In **Assignments & Clips**, set whether this story needs an **Article**, an **Instagram Reel**, or **Both**.
2. Click **Create Assignment & Generate Draft**. This creates a new versioned package in the local database and switches directly to review.

### Step 3: Visuals & Video Footage
* **If you have real footage**: Go to **Review & Revisions → Reel → Clips & Timing** and upload your MP4 file. The player immediately binds it across all scenes.
* **If you need an illustrative visual**: Open **AI Media Studio**, select the **Finnish Winter** preset, generate your image or video with Google Veo, and click **`🎬 Apply as Reel Video Background`** or **`📌 Apply as Article Featured Image`**.

### Step 4: Previewing & Safe-Area Verification
1. On the **Reel** tab, click **Play** to test the pacing (9s, 12s, or 16s).
2. Toggle the **Safe Area Guide** to verify that:
   * The solid white river-heart logo is in the top-left margin.
   * The `#003882` navy category badge is visible.
   * The headline text stays within the central 4:5 Instagram feed crop.
3. If adjustments are needed, use **Feedback & Revision** to request changes or attach new clips.

### Step 5: Independent Component Approval & Publishing
1. Approve each component independently:
   * Click **Approve Article** (re-runs the duplicate check against `ilovefinland.net`).
   * Click **Approve Reel** (locks the script and timing).
   * Click **Approve Static Post**.
2. Copy ready fields using the **`📋 Copy`** buttons or push to WordPress.
3. Check WordPress at `ilovefinland.net/wp-admin/` for a final visual check before setting the post to Public.

## 4. Key Best Practices
1. **Keep 1 Active Focus Story at a Time**: Complete and approve the active package (currently Package #43: *Winter Tyre Change*) before launching a new assignment.
2. **Center Video Motion**: When filming with a smartphone or generating with AI, keep the primary subject in the vertical center so it looks natural in both 9:16 full-screen and 4:5 grid views.
3. **Use Version History**: Every revision submitted creates a clean version snapshot (e.g., Version 6 → Version 7). You can switch back anytime in the version dropdown.
